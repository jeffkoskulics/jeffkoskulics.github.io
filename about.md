Physicist and engineer in the greater Phoenix area.

**Now** — Senior Field Applications Engineer at PDF Solutions (eProbe),
working in semiconductor metrology, yield, and process control.

**Before** — Senior Product Development Engineer and Product Development
Manager at Blink Charging (2021–2026), building hardware for EV charging and
energy storage: optics, sensors, PCB, firmware, systems integration, and the
handoff from prototype to pilot manufacturing.

**Training** — Ph.D. in physics, Stevens Institute of Technology.

**Tools I reach for** — design of experiments, statistical process control,
root cause and failure analysis, signal processing, MATLAB, Python, SolidWorks.

**Interests** — optical and sensor metrology, semiconductor process control,
energy storage and power electronics, test and measurement, and building small
reliable systems that keep running when nobody is watching.
